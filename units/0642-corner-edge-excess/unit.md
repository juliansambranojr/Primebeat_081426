**Question.** Julian's answer to the first corner question (`notes/corner-questions.md`): follow up the edge excess of unit 0640 (corner-lower-bounds), where cells below a twentieth of their root-square size came about twice as often as independent phases predict on two edges. More edges, deeper, and whether the excess holds as zeros are added.

**What ran.** `run/edge.py`, by `run/run.sh`, through `lab run`, on Odlyzko's `edge.zeros` 2001052 zeros (`imported/odlyzko/zeros6`, committed by the Mac session; this session checked its hash, its count and that its first hundred thousand equal the table unit 0640 (corner-lower-bounds) and unit 0641 (corner-residues) used). Twelve edges, the row the depth plus one to the depth plus sixty-four, to depth six hundred, and two rays as controls, `edge.cells` 7080 cells inside `Capstone.OpenCorner`. Three thresholds, a fiftieth, a twentieth and a tenth of the root-square size; three benchmarks for the count below each, a Gaussian, the largest term at its own amplitude over a Gaussian rest, and the fifty largest terms at their own amplitudes over a Gaussian rest, by Monte Carlo; and two checks of independence, the runs of consecutive small cells along each edge and how often neighbouring edges share their small cells.

**The excess is real in count, the same at every threshold, and it holds as zeros are added.** Over the twelve edges, `edge.edges_total.0.05.observed` 309 cells fall below a twentieth, against `edge.edges_total.0.05.top50` 239.2 for the fifty-term benchmark (`edge.edges_total.0.05.gauss` 252.0 for the Gaussian); below a fiftieth `edge.edges_total.0.02.observed` 119 against `edge.edges_total.0.02.top50` 95.5; below a tenth `edge.edges_total.0.1.observed` 629 against `edge.edges_total.0.1.top50` 477.5. That is the same factor near a third over expectation at all three, so the density near zero is raised evenly rather than piled at zero. On the edge at depth plus one the count is `edge.diagonals.edge1.t0.05.observed_K100000` 28 with a hundred thousand zeros, `edge.diagonals.edge1.t0.05.observed_K500000` 27 with five hundred thousand and `edge.diagonals.edge1.t0.05.observed` 27 with all of them, against `edge.diagonals.edge1.t0.05.expected_top50` 19.3; on the edge at depth plus twelve `edge.diagonals.edge12.t0.05.observed_K100000` 27, `edge.diagonals.edge12.t0.05.observed_K500000` 29, `edge.diagonals.edge12.t0.05.observed` 29, against `edge.diagonals.edge12.t0.05.expected_top50` 19.6.

**The amplitudes do not explain it.** The fifty largest terms carry a median `edge.diagonals.edge1.top50_share_of_square_median` 0.965 of a cell's square on the edge at depth plus one, and the runner-up is a median `edge.diagonals.edge1.top2_ratio_median` 0.932 of the largest; keeping all fifty at their own amplitudes lowers the expectation below the Gaussian's. What is left is the phases.

**The near edges are one observation, and the small cells come in runs.** Of the small cells on the edge at depth plus one, `edge.neighbour_edges_shared.1_2.share` 0.815 sit within one depth of a small cell on the edge at depth plus two, against `edge.neighbour_edges_shared.1_2.chance` 0.165 by chance; from depth plus three to plus four `edge.neighbour_edges_shared.3_4.share` 0.846 against `edge.neighbour_edges_shared.3_4.chance` 0.182, from plus four to plus six `edge.neighbour_edges_shared.4_6.share` 0.419. From plus eight on the share is at chance: `edge.neighbour_edges_shared.8_12.share` 0.111 against `edge.neighbour_edges_shared.8_12.chance` 0.167, `edge.neighbour_edges_shared.12_16.share` 0.138 against `edge.neighbour_edges_shared.12_16.chance` 0.16, `edge.neighbour_edges_shared.24_32.share` 0.111 against `edge.neighbour_edges_shared.24_32.chance` 0.139. Along one edge the small cells fall in runs: `edge.diagonals.edge1.t0.05.observed` 27 cells in `edge.diagonals.edge1.t0.05.runs` 9 runs on the edge at depth plus one, `edge.diagonals.edge12.t0.05.observed` 29 in `edge.diagonals.edge12.t0.05.runs` 9 at plus twelve.

**Far from the corner's edge it fades.** At depth plus forty-eight `edge.diagonals.edge48.t0.05.observed` 18 against `edge.diagonals.edge48.t0.05.expected_top50` 20.9, at plus sixty-four `edge.diagonals.edge64.t0.05.observed` 17 against `edge.diagonals.edge64.t0.05.expected_top50` 21.7; the ray at three times the depth has `edge.diagonals.ray3.0.t0.05.observed` 18 against `edge.diagonals.ray3.0.t0.05.expected_top50` 14.8, in `edge.diagonals.ray3.0.t0.05.runs` 15 runs.

**What it shows.** Near the corner's edge, out to the edge at depth plus thirty-two or so, a corner cell lands near zero about a third more often than independent phases with the true amplitudes allow, at every scale measured and with every zero up to two million counted. The excess is carried by the phases, comes in runs of about three neighbouring cells, and the edges up to depth plus eight share it, so the twelve edges are fewer independent observations than they look; the four edges from plus twelve to plus thirty-two, which do not share, each show it (`edge.diagonals.edge16.t0.05.observed` 28 against `edge.diagonals.edge16.t0.05.expected_top50` 19.9 at plus sixteen, `edge.diagonals.edge24.t0.05.observed` 27 against `edge.diagonals.edge24.t0.05.expected_top50` 20.1 at plus twenty-four, `edge.diagonals.edge32.t0.05.observed` 25 against `edge.diagonals.edge32.t0.05.expected_top50` 20.6 at plus thirty-two). It points the opposite way from a lower bound: near the edge, the corner's sums come close to zero more often than chance. Exploratory; no outcome marked.
