**Question.** Two items from the audit. Section `record.fourteen` 14 answered NO on one sideband; does the answer stand on the term itself, `−(h/2)²·Re((S₊ + S₋)²)` with both sidebands (`WeilPowerBridge.term_eq_sidebands`)? And section `record.thirteen` 13 left a gap-dependent `L(ε, T, δ)` open: what does it cost, on the same object, and which quantifier of the rung does it change?

**What ran.** `run/two_sideband.py` evaluates `S m w = cS m · sinh w / QS m w` (`WeilOddPower.S_mul_QS`) exactly in `mpmath` at both sidebands `w± = (ρ − 1/2 ± iγ)h`, with `γ` an actual zero height from the bench's table of `record.zeros_table` 600 zeros, and compares the sign of the two-sideband term with the one-sideband sign over a grid of `two_sideband.grid_points` 1176 points of the box `ε' ∈ (0, 1/2]`, `Δ ∈ [−1/2, 1/2]`, at `record.heights` 3 heights and `record.supports` 2 supports, `λ = ` `record.lam` 1. `run/gap_price.py` evaluates `|S|` exactly in log space and finds the least `h` at which the target's term beats the on-line background (`WeilPowerOnLine.onLineBound_le` with `lowCount` at `record.lowcount_used` 0, its true value), the cluster (`15 log T + 73` members with `ε' ≤ ε` and `δ ≤ |Δ| < 1/2`, each at its worst), and the far zeros (the band's count, `112 log T + 698` past the main term, each at `|Δ| = 1/2`). Output in `run/*.log`; the cited numbers in `run/*.json`.

**What it shows.**

The sign. On the tree's identity the target's own term is negative, and a member at the target's own height with the same real part has the target's sign: `two_sideband.member_at_height_sign` −1, it helps. Unit `record.unit_window` 0360 wrote "hurts when `cos 2A > 0`", which is the other sign; its two conclusions stand, since the hurting and helping stripes alternate and the measure argument is symmetric, but its stated reason, a zero at the target's height always hurting, is wrong, and its item on `σ = 0` calling the sign fixed is wrong too (`(ε'² − Δ²)/(ε'² + Δ²)` is negative past `Δ = ε'`). Block 13g's `term_nonneg` (unit `record.unit_near` 0354) had the sign right: the near members help.

Section 14 on the term. At every grid point the two-sideband term has the one-sideband sign: `two_sideband.disagree_max` 0 disagreements. The far sideband is at most `two_sideband.ratio_max` 6.3e-51 of the near one across the table, at `γ` from `record.gamma_first` 14.1347 to `record.gamma_600` 939.024 and `h` at `record.h_lo` 20 and `record.h_hi` 60. The hurting fraction of the box is `two_sideband.hurt_frac_h20` 0.7024 at the small support and `two_sideband.hurt_frac_h60` 0.6395 at the larger, on its way to the half that 0360 computed in the limit. So section 14's NO is verified on the term: no window of this shape fixes the off-line sign, and the death it names is the same as section 13's, uniformity over configurations.

The gap-dependent range. `gap_price.cells` 45 cells, `ε ∈ {1/2, 1/4, 1/10}`, `T ∈ {10³, 10⁶, 10¹²}`, `δ ∈ {1, 0.3, 0.1, 0.03, 0.01}`, and `gap_price.cells_closed` 45 close. At `ε = 1/4`, `T = 10⁶`: `h₀ = ` `gap_price.h0_e025_T6_d01` 5995 for every `δ ≥ 0.1`, where the background binds; `gap_price.h0_e025_T6_d003` 31176 at `δ = 0.03` and `gap_price.h0_e025_T6_d001` 279948 at `δ = 0.01`, where the cluster binds, with `h₀·δ²` at `gap_price.h0_delta2_limit` 28. `gap_price.cells_cluster_bound` 19 of the cells are cluster-bound. The worst cell, `ε = 1/10`, `T = 10¹²`, `δ = 0.01`, is `gap_price.h0_e01_T12_d001` 306227, support `L = 2h₀ = ` `record.L_e01_T12_d001` 612454. So `L(ε, T, δ)` is polynomial in `log T`, `1/ε²` and `1/δ²`: crude, explicit, and it closes with the modules that stand plus block 13j.

What the hypothesis is. Members with `|Δ| ≤ lo/b`, `lo = λπ³/(8ε')`, help at every `h ≤ b` (block 13g); at the `ε = 1/4`, `T = 10⁶`, `δ = 0.03` cell that radius is `gap_price.lo_over_h0_e025_T6_d003` 4.97e-4 in units of height. The gap-dependent theorem therefore assumes: no other off-line zero of the box has height distance from the target in `(lo/b, δ)`. That is the quantifier it changes. The rung's `∀ configurations` at `L(ε, T)` becomes `∀ configurations with no member in that window` at `L(ε, T, δ)`; the death of the uniform `L` (unit `record.unit_audit` 0361) is untouched.

What consumes it. Nothing in the tree. `IsTest L G` is monotone in `L` (the support condition is an inclusion), so positivity at a support `L*` covers every `L ≤ L*`, and the arrow `box_empty_of_positive_of_detect` then gives a dichotomy rather than an empty box: either no zero in the box, or two off-line zeros of the box within `δ*` in height, with `δ*` the gap at which `L(ε, T, δ*) = L*`. Whether that dichotomy is worth a Lean module is Julian's call; the price of the module is the assembly of the background, comparison and selection sections (`§ 6`, `§ 7`, `§ 8`) and block `13j` under the gap hypothesis, with block 13g for the near members and `zeta_local_zero_count` for the cluster count.

**Decision.** Section 14's mark moves to NO, verified on the two-sideband term, with 0360's sign label corrected in its text. Section 13's gap-dependent `L` moves from OPEN to PRICED, conditional, with the hypothesis and the table above, nothing consuming it. Both marks move in this commit on a script that computes on `term_eq_sidebands`'s object, as `DESIGN.md` v10 asks. Unit 0360 is left as written and pointed at from here.
