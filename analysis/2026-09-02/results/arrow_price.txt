explicit formula by x^eps. Requiring x^eps <= K gives the budget
eps_max(x, K) = log K / log x. Entry 130's precedent accepts
constants 70x-700x worse than the literature's.

==============================================================================
The bench's far-tail bound assumes |N(T) - Nbar(T)| <= Rmax(T) with
  Rmax = 0.137 log T + 0.443 log log T + 4.35 (assumed)
Upstream states exactly that Prop as
  riemannZeta.Riemann_vonMangoldt_bound b1 b2 b3
  = forall T >= 2, |N T - (T/2pi log(T/2pi) - T/2pi + 7/8)|
      <= b1 log T + b2 log log T + b3
  (ZetaDefinitions.lean:149-162), and instantiates it at
  backlund_bound : Riemann_vonMangoldt_bound 0.137 0.443 6.1
  (Kadiri.lean:2618, proof `sorry` at :2619).
The first two constants agree; the third does not: the bench assumed
4.35 where the upstream statement carries 6.1. So the assumed form is
SHARPER than what upstream would supply. Cost of using 6.1 instead:
