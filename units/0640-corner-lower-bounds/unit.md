**Question.** The first lead the handoff names: a lower bound for the corner's almost-periodic sums, the comb's leader plus the near-harmonic family (unit 0609, corner-harmonics, through unit 0615, harmonic-alignment), at every cell along a diagonal whose leaders change, and whether any such bound is in reach, with more zeros than the two thousand used before.

**What ran.** `run/diag.py`, by `run/run.sh`, through `lab run`. A cell is Riemann's R, differenced, less each zero's explicit-formula term, differenced, over the first K of Odlyzko's `diag.zeros` 100000 zeros, the highest at `diag.gamma_max` 74920.8. Odlyzko's site is refused by this machine's network, so the zeros are the table already in the tree. First the model is checked against the exact table on unit 0597's corner, `diag.validation.cells` 2962 cells. Then it is carried past row ninety-two along eight diagonals inside `Capstone.OpenCorner` to depth four hundred and twenty: five rays, the row a fixed multiple of the depth, and three edges, the row the depth plus one, eight or thirty-two. Along each the comb's leader changes from the zero at `diag.leader_changes.d67[0]` 14.135 to the one at `diag.leader_changes.d67[1]` 40.919 at depth sixty-seven, and to the one at `diag.leader_changes.d354[1]` 49.774 at depth three hundred and fifty-four. Each cell is carried divided by its scale, the size of a zero term at the top of the comb; a first run without that overflowed past row six hundred and was discarded.

**The model holds where it can be checked.** With all hundred thousand zeros it gets the sign of `diag.validation.K100000.signs` 2932 of the corner's cells, against `diag.validation.K2000.signs` 2921 with two thousand, at a median relative error of `diag.validation.K100000.median_rel_err` 0.0273. The cells it misses nearly cancel: their median size is `diag.validation.wrong_cells_true_over_sigma_median` 0.0111 of the root-square size of the zero terms, against `diag.validation.all_cells_true_over_sigma_median` 0.5993 over all cells. Past row ninety-two it is stable to a few percent: between thirty thousand and a hundred thousand zeros the cells on the ray at twice the depth move a median `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.move_over_cell_median` 0.0285 of their size, and `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.sign_changes_30k_to_100k` 11 of its `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.cells` 374 cells change sign.

**No cell has a dominant leader.** The absolute sum of the other zeros' terms over the leader's has median `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.dominance_deficit_median.K2000` 7.86 on that ray with two thousand zeros, `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.dominance_deficit_median.K10000` 12.12 with ten thousand, `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.dominance_deficit_median.K30000` 15.59 with thirty thousand and `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.dominance_deficit_median.K100000` 19.91 with a hundred thousand, and it keeps growing as zeros are added. On the edge at depth plus one it runs `diag.diagonals.edge1.dominance_deficit_median.K2000` 7.63 to `diag.diagonals.edge1.dominance_deficit_median.K100000` 19.37. The leader outweighs the rest in `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.cells_leader_dominates_K100000` 0 cells on the ray and `diag.diagonals.edge1.cells_leader_dominates_K100000` 0 on the edge. A bound of the form leader less everything else is out at every cell.

**In root-square size the leader is the rest's size, on every diagonal.** The leader over the root-square size of the rest has median `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.leader_over_rest_rms_median` 1.084 on the ray at twice the depth, `diag.diagonals.ray3.3.leader_over_rest_rms_median` 1.085 on the steepest ray, `diag.diagonals.edge1.leader_over_rest_rms_median` 1.088 on the edge. At the first change of leader it dips to `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.at_change_d67.leader_over_rest_rms` 0.834, where the old leader is `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.at_change_d67.old_over_new_amplitude` 0.972 of the new; at the second it is `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.at_change_d354.leader_over_rest_rms` 1.067. The leader and the near-harmonic family give every sign the model gives, `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.sign_from_leader_and_family` 374 of the ray's cells and `diag.diagonals.edge1.sign_from_leader_and_family` 329 of the edge's `diag.diagonals.edge1.cells` 329; the leader alone gives `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.sign_from_leader_alone` 262 and `diag.diagonals.edge1.sign_from_leader_alone` 213. The smooth part is negligible: its median over the root-square size is `diag.diagonals.ray3.3.smooth_over_sigma_median` 2.54e-08 even on the steepest ray.

**Small cells come at the random rate on the rays, and more often on the edges.** The cells below a twentieth of their root-square size number `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.cells_below_0.05sigma` 20 on the ray at twice the depth, against `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.expected_below_0.05sigma_leader_plus_gaussian` 14.4 for a leader at an independent phase over a Gaussian rest; `diag.diagonals.ray3.0.cells_below_0.05sigma` 15 against `diag.diagonals.ray3.0.expected_below_0.05sigma_leader_plus_gaussian` 15.0 at three times the depth; `diag.diagonals.ray1.5.cells_below_0.05sigma` 14 against `diag.diagonals.ray1.5.expected_below_0.05sigma_leader_plus_gaussian` 13.8 at one and a half. On the edges they are about twice as common: `diag.diagonals.edge1.cells_below_0.05sigma` 25 against `diag.diagonals.edge1.expected_below_0.05sigma_leader_plus_gaussian` 12.6 at depth plus one, `diag.diagonals.edge8.cells_below_0.05sigma` 26 against `diag.diagonals.edge8.expected_below_0.05sigma_leader_plus_gaussian` 12.9 at depth plus eight. The smallest cell on the ray is `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.min_cell_over_sigma` 0.00025 of its size, at row `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.argmin_cell[0]` 242, depth `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.argmin_cell[1]` 121, where independent phases expect a minimum near `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.expected_min_iid` 0.00335.

**A truncated explicit formula would need zeros past any table.** By the order estimate in the script, the zero height that would fix a cell's sign is ten to the `diag.diagonals.edge1.log10_T_needed.min` 13.65 at the cheapest cell measured, on the edge at depth plus one, a median ten to the `diag.diagonals.ray2.0.log10_T_needed.median` 63.75 on the ray at twice the depth, and it grows by `diag.diagonals.edge1.log10_T_needed.slope_per_depth` 0.0996 decades per depth on that edge and `diag.diagonals.ray3.3.log10_T_needed.slope_per_depth` 0.4463 on the steepest ray. The zeros in hand reach ten to the `diag.log10_gamma_max` 4.87.

**What it shows.** Along diagonals where the leader changes, a corner cell is a leader of the same root-square size as the rest, with the rest's absolute sum growing without limit as zeros are added: no dominance bound exists at any cell. The small cells arrive at the rate a sum with independent phases gives, so the near-harmonic family's beat does not keep the cells away from zero on the rays, and on the edges the small cells come about twice as often. The bound the lead asks for would have to come from something these sums do not show, an arithmetic fact about the phases at the integer cells. Where the lead stands: no pointwise lower bound is in reach by size, by root-square size or by truncation. Exploratory; no outcome marked.
