1 -> 2 zeros gains 0.0363.  41 -> 57 zeros gains only 0.0168.
The fraction tops out at 0.250 - at Nyquist 158.65, 57 zeros and 1308 samples,
75% of the signal is still aliased content folded down.

Each zero contributes roughly 1/gamma^2 to the variance, so the series
over zeros converges and the reachable fraction has a limit.  Refining
the base cannot buy past it.  Section 8's lever is real but bounded.
