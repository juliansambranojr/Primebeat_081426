**What was found.** It does not close as sketched. From `re_S_sq_ge` (unit `unit_phase`) and `uLam_bracket` (unit `unit_ulam`), a member at `ζ = ε' + iΔ` contributes, relative to the target at `ε`, `A·cos θ` with `A = ((ε'² + Δ²)/ε²)·exp(2(ε'² − Δ² − ε²)u)`, `θ = 4ε'Δ·u + arg ζ²` and `u ≈ h/(λπ²)`. Detection at `h` needs `1 + Σ A_k cos θ_k` above the background and error terms. Section 13 splits members by `Δ·h₀` under `split_dh` 7, calling the terms below negative because the phase `phase_per` 0.2 per unit of `Δ·h` is under `phase_h0` 1.4 radians. That holds at `h` near `h₀` only; over the range to `H` the phase reaches `1.4·H/h₀` and those members turn. Splitting by `Δ·H` instead makes the Abel bound of the far members `π³λ/(4ε'Δ)`, about `1.1λH/ε'` each, the size of the range.

The shell in between is the killer. A member with the target's real part and small `Δ` has `A ≤ 1` (once `u ≥ 1/(2ε²)`) and no decay over a range `[X, 2X]` when `Δ` is below about `λ/(εX)`; at phase `π` it cancels the target. Zeros placed at heights in geometric progression of ratio `shell_ratio` 1.5 put about two members in the hurting lobe `[2π/3, 4π/3]` at every `h`, so every range fails. The adversary needs one shell per factor `1.5` of range, and the tree's count `zeta_local_zero_count` allows `band_a` 15 times the log of the height plus `band_b` 73 of them. So `H/a` is at most `T'` raised to `15·ln 1.5`, an exponent above `expo` 6, with `T' = T + H/(8K'π²λ)` the target's height after section 8's walk. The range outgrows the height it has to fit inside: unit `unit_prev`'s circularity again, at a smaller exponent. No weight shape, range centre, or `λ` escapes it: the phase `4ε'Δu` is the analytic continuation of a real even transform off the line, Weil's pairing of `ρ` with `1 − ρ̄` gives `2 Re S(w)²`, and aligning the shell's phases by choice of range or centre is Dirichlet, the death of section 9.

**What closes it.** A short-interval zero count with leading constant below `c1_max` 2.47, which is `1/ln 1.5`. The count in a window of height under one is then about `2B₁ log T`, and the shell is handled by pigeonhole over the ratio-`1.5` shells with exponent `2B₁·ln 1.5`, below one exactly when `B₁ < b1_max` 1.23. Backlund's constant is `backlund` 0.137 and Trudgian's is `trudgian` 0.112, so the mathematics closes. The tree's crude chain gives `B₁ = a·15` with Backlund's step constant `a` measured at `step_a` 0.462 in O77, so about `b1_crude` 7, against a census budget of `budget` 100 (`rvM_of_sFromLocal`); the step `StmtSFromLocal` itself is still open.
